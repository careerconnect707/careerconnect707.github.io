P2P Customer Support Specialist

Full-time remote customer communication and support position.

Role Responsibilities

  • Communicate with customers professionally through approved online communication channels.
  • Respond to customer enquiries and provide clear information based on company procedures.
  • Maintain accurate communication records where required.
  • Escalate unresolved or sensitive cases to the appropriate supervisor.
  • Follow privacy, security and customer service guidelines.

Candidate Requirements

  • Reliable computer or laptop and a stable internet connection.
  • Ability to communicate clearly in English.
  • Responsible, patient and comfortable assisting customers online.
  • Availability for the working schedule confirmed during screening.
  • Customer support experience is helpful but not automatically required.

Compensation Information

The advertised compensation is 3,000 USDT per month plus commission, subject to successful screening, written employment terms, performance requirements and the applicable payment schedule. Applicants should review and understand all written terms before accepting an offer.

Applicant Safety Notice

Career Connect does not charge applicants a recruitment, registration, training or security-deposit fee. Applicants should never send money, share wallet recovery phrases, disclose banking passwords or purchase cryptocurrency as a condition of receiving a job offer. Please verify all written employment terms before accepting a position.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important information for applicants.

Is there an application or recruitment fee?

No. Applicants should not be asked to pay a recruitment, registration, interview, training or security-deposit fee.

Does applying guarantee that I will be hired?

No. Applications are subject to review, screening and the requirements of the available position.

Is previous customer service experience required?

Relevant experience can be an advantage. Final eligibility is determined during the screening process.

How is compensation confirmed?

Compensation, commission conditions, payment frequency and employment requirements should be provided in writing before work begins.

Will applicants be asked for sensitive financial information?

Applicants should never provide wallet recovery phrases, online banking passwords, payment authentication codes or unrestricted access to personal financial accounts.

How will shortlisted applicants be contacted?

Shortlisted applicants will receive further instructions through the communication details provided during the application process.

Ready to Review the Opportunity?

Read all position information carefully before beginning your application. Do not send payment or sensitive financial credentials to anyone claiming to guarantee employment.
